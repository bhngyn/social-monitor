How to use

  • Search looks through post text, video titles and descriptions, YouTube captions, speech transcribed by Whisper, text read off video frames and photos (OCR), platform image descriptions, Instagram location tags and comments. Several words must all appear in the same passage. Accents, case and vowel marks are ignored. With spelling variants & near matches on (the default), a watchlist name also finds all its recorded spellings (and native-script forms), and other words of four or more letters also find near spellings in the archive: transliteration swaps such as kh/ch/h or tz/ts/z, doubled letters, and small typos. The line under the search box shows every extra spelling used. Put a phrase in "quotes" to match it exactly.
  • Places lists every entry in the project's watchlist with how many items mention it. Names that are also common words or first names are ambiguous: hidden unless you tick the box, and always ranked below clear matches.
  • Click any result to open the item. Click a timestamp to jump the video there.
  • Tag items relevant / needs review / not relevant with a note. Tags are saved in this browser only. Use My review → Export CSV to share them; Import CSV merges a colleague's file.

What the fields mean

Machine transcripts and OCR contain errors. Always confirm against the video or photo before relying on a hit. See METHODOLOGY.md for sources, dates, tools and known gaps.
